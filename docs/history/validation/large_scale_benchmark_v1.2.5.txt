Limage v1.2.5 large-scale geometry benchmark
============================================
Synthetic sparse regular post-stack SEG-Y:
  logical size: 1.2480036 GB
  geometry: 200 Inline x 1000 Crossline = 200,000 traces
  ns: 1500, dt: 2 ms, IEEE float32

Same local test environment:
  v1.2.4 exhaustive geometry build: 150.884953 ms
  v1.2.5 validated fast-grid build:   11.639326 ms
  v1.2.5 geometry headers touched:    127
  observed geometry speedup:          ~12.96x

Important: v1.2.5 also removes the eager full-volume Time-Slice slab load when
the active mode is Inline or Crossline. That I/O removal is not included in
the 12.96x geometry-only number and can dominate the real first-open gain on
multi-million-trace field volumes.
