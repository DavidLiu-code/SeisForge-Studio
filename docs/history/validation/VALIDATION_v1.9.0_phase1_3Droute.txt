Limage v1.9.0 Phase 1.1 3-D route validation
Date: 2026-08-24

Observed failure:
  %SEISFORGE_TEST_ROUTE_FILE% was routed by KindAuto to Line2D even though the same
  file subsequently built a valid Regular3D index in the Volume workspace.

Root cause measured on the actual attachment:
  1600 headers: IL/XL 193/25, score 89.171, overall confidence 0.553,
                best-candidate confidence 0.580 (rounded).
  3000 headers: IL/XL 21/25, score 90.625, overall confidence 0.562,
                best-candidate confidence 0.589.
  Inline values are duplicated at trace-header bytes 21 and 193. The overall
  confidence penalized the tied byte locations even though both describe the
  same strong regular grid.

Fix:
  - KindAuto now samples 3000 headers, matching the Volume worker.
  - The unchanged score >= 75 and confidence >= 0.58 thresholds are applied
    to the best candidate's grid confidence when it is higher than the
    byte-selection confidence.
  - workspace_trace.log now records score and effective confidence.

Actual attachment verification after the fix:

  IL/XL: 21/25
  Score: 90.625
